## Checkpoints per Short

- **2 min** — diagnostic only, no decision. Low numbers here are normal.
- **24h** — real signal. <100 views = distribution problem, not content problem.
- **72h** — Shorts can still surface late; judge before deleting anything.

Watch **viewed vs swiped away** in the first 2 seconds, not raw view count. A Short that gets pushed and rejected shows high impressions with low views; a Short nobody sees shows no impressions at all.
