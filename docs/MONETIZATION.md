# Vsad Studios: Monetization

## Revenue Streams

| Stream | Source | Target (Month 3) | Target (Month 6+) |
|--------|--------|------------------|-------------------|
| Affiliate revenue | 10% rev-share from featured partners | $500-1000/mo | $2-5K/mo |
| YouTube AdSense | Views on UGC template videos | $500-1000/mo | $1.5-3K/mo |
| Premium custom videos | $500-1000/video | 1-2/mo | 4-8/mo |
| Agency retainers | $1000-3000/mo per client | 0-1 | 2-3 |
| Sponsorships | Featured playlists / community posts | $0 | $500-2000/mo |
| Template sales / white-label | Editable packs for agencies | $0 | $500-1500/mo |

## Affiliate Model (Core)

1. Free UGC video + editable template for the brand
2. Brand opts into 10% monthly revenue share
3. Brand links back to Vsad Studios channel (backlinks = SEO)
4. Tracked in `data/affiliate-partners.md` + `data/brand-submissions.json`

## Tiers

- **Tier 1 — Free Users:** download, use with watermark, no commitment
- **Tier 2 — Affiliate Partners:** 10% share, custom branded versions, priority uploads
- **Tier 3 — Agency Partners:** $1000-3000/mo retainer, white-label templates, bulk production

## Rules

- 10% is offered, never forced
- Monthly reconciliation, payments tracked explicitly
- Revenue projections stay conservative until real data lands
