# Voiceover Script Template

Shorts ad VO for the "Free Product Ads" series. One consistent narrator voice across the whole series — pick one character in Google Flow and reuse it every time (same voice = channel identity).

## Format (fixed, 10s video)

| Time | Line |
|------|------|
| 0.0–0.3s | Silent beat — visuals already moving, VO not yet |
| 0.3s | "Free product ads, part [N]." |
| 1.2s | "Today — [product name]." |
| 2.0s | "Ten seconds. Free to download." |
| 2.8s | VO stops — music/visuals carry the rest |

VO total ≈ 3s, ends before the hero shot. Never talk over the logo reveal.

## Voice settings (Google Flow)

- Character: pick one narrator, reuse always
- Tone: warm, confident, conversational — not salesy hype
- Pace: slightly faster than normal speech, no dramatic pauses
- Level: -6 dB under music bed (music ducks under VO)

## Rules

- 0.3s delay is fixed — the visual hook lands before any speech
- Say the product name as the brand writes it (no invented names, no wrong products on screen)
- Burn the VO into the video in CapCut/Resolve; also export a clean no-VO version for the template package
